# Resume Tailoring Overview
> *This prompt focuses on building and tailoring resumes. This is just an overview of what the prompt is for, more details about the prompt can be found on [`prompt.md`](./prompt.md). *
## Overview
This prompt produces a well made resume based off work experience and specific job postings you want to apply for. This prompt saves endless amounts of time used on rewriting applications for different jobs. This can be useful for job seekers and career switchers. 
**Best for:**
- Tailoring a professional resume
- Emphasizing transferable skills
- **Structure:** BAB (Before, After, Bridge)
- **Technique:** few-shot 
- **Output:** Short and concise one to two pages resume
---
## Quick Start
1) Have the relevant information needed for your job posting 
2) Apply your information in a resume. This can be found on [`examples/`](./examples/). Click on 'example1'
and there you will see an example of what a resume should look like. 

---
## Customization Tips
- **Want more detail?** Elaborate on your skills that are relevant to your job posting, however if you possess skills that don't relate to the job but you think it can somehow strength your application, elaborate on how your unrelated skills can still help you in your job posting. 
- **Want it shorter?** Prompt Gemini to exclude any unnecessary or repetitive information in your resume. Use bullet points to shorten material. 
- **Different context?** Focus on information regarding your skills that can apply to your job posting. 
---
## Technical Details
- **Structure:** BAB
- **Technique:** Few shot
- **Best models:** Advanced LLMs (e.g., GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro)
- **Placeholders:** 3 main placeholders ([Weak resume], [Professional resume], [Job seekers, transfers])
